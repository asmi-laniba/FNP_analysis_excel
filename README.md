# Ferns and Petals (FNP) Sales Analysis – Excel Dashboard Project  

##  Project Overview

**Project Title**: FNP Sales Analysis  
**Level**: Beginner  

This project analyzes the sales data of **Ferns and Petals (FNP)** — a company that delivers gifts for special occasions like **Diwali, Raksha Bandhan, Holi, Valentine’s Day, Birthdays, and Anniversaries**.  

The aim is to gain insights into **sales trends**, **customer spending patterns**, and **product performance** using an interactive **Excel Dashboard**.

---

##  Problem Statement
FNP needs to understand its sales performance across products, cities, and occasions.  
You are provided with datasets containing information about **customers**, **orders**, and **products**.  

The objective is to create a dashboard answering key business questions:

1. What is the **total revenue**?  
2. What is the **average order-to-delivery time**?  
3. How do **monthly sales** fluctuate?  
4. Which are the **top 5 products by revenue**?  
5. What is the **average customer spending**?  
6. Which are the **top cities** by order count?  
7. How does **revenue vary across occasions**?  
8. Is there a link between **order quantity and delivery time**?  
9. Which **occasions** generate the most revenue?  
10. What are the **top-performing categories**?

---

##  Dataset Description
Three CSV files were used for analysis:

| **File Name**   | **Description**                                                                                     |
|---------------- |-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| `customers.csv` | Customer details like ID, name, and city.                                                           |
| `orders.csv`    | Includes order ID, customer ID, product ID, order date, delivery date, quantity, and total revenue. |
| `products.csv`  | Contains product ID, category, and product name.                                                    |

 All files are stored in the folder: `fnp datasets/`

##  Dashboard Insights

### **Key Metrics**
-  **Total Orders Placed:** 1000  
-  **Total Revenue:** ₹35,20,984  
-  **Average Order-to-Delivery Time:** 5.53 days  
-  **Average Customer Spend:** ₹3,520.98  

### **Dashboard Visuals**
- **Revenue by Occasion** – Compare total revenue across special events.  
- **Revenue by Category** – Track performance of product categories like Cakes, Gifts, and Sweets.  
- **Revenue by Hour (Order Time)** – Identify peak ordering hours.  
- **Revenue by Month** – Observe monthly sales fluctuations.  
- **Top 5 Products by Revenue** – List of best-selling items.  
- **Top 10 Cities by Orders** – Cities contributing the most orders.  
- **Order Quantity vs Delivery Time** – Relationship between order size and delivery time.  

### **Dashboard Filters**
- Order Date  
- Delivery Date  
- Occasion  

---

##  Key Insights
- **Valentine’s Day** and **Anniversary** generate the highest sales.  
- **Gift items** and **Cakes** dominate revenue contribution.  
- **Major cities** like Bhubaneswar and Bhopal lead in order count.  
- Average **delivery time** is around **5.5 days**.  
- Average **customer spend** ≈ ₹3,500 per order.
